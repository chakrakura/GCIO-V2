Frequently asked

Can I download or print reports?

To protect proprietary research, reports are read-only within the portal. Download and print are disabled; use the in-browser viewer and the Save list to keep track of what matters.

How do I reset my password?

Use “Forgot password” on the login screen, or Change password under Profile → Account Security. A reset link is sent to your registered email.

Who can see my saved reports?

Your saved list is private to your account and never shared with other users in your organisation.

How is new research delivered?

New research appears on your Home feed immediately. You can also opt into daily, weekly, and monthly emails under Communication Preferences.
